Qwen layer 0: rows of the LLM's input embedding table (4-bit); a word is the mean of its token rows. Any word made of ASCII letters.
Qwen layers 12 / 24: Qwen3.5-2B's hidden state for Word: <word> halfway through (layer 12 of 24) and after the last layer, at the word's last token. Precomputed for a list of ~100 words (see list); other words are left out of these two views only.
mdbr-leaf-ir: MongoDB's 23M-parameter retrieval model (Aug 2025), run with transformers.js; each word or phrase goes through the full model and comes out as a unit-length 768-d vector. Downloads ~46 MB (fp16) from Hugging Face on first use.
Each view is zoomed so its farthest reference point sits at the same distance (the spaces differ in scale by up to ~250×): compare shapes, and read the numbers below each view for the actual values.

What the vectors look like

mdbr-leaf-ir vectors drawn as images: 768 values as a 24 × 32 grid, dimension i at row i // 32, column i % 32. Blue is negative, red positive, brighter means larger.

About 95% of every single-word vector is the same "an English word" component, so it is removed first: each word has the mean of 1000 common English words subtracted.

The top rows are brightest in every image: the model is trained so that its first dimensions carry the most information (it still works when the vector is cut short). The dimension order is otherwise arbitrary, so related words do not form shapes; their similarity shows in numbers instead. After centering, cosine king · queen = 0.54, (king − man + woman) · queen = 0.60, but apple · king = 0.12 and apple · (man − woman) = −0.02.
